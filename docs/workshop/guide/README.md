# Workshop Guide (Step by Step)

Welcome. In this workshop you will build and launch your own three-tier website: a public catalog with inquiry forms, a membership area with payment receipts, donations, and Admin/Staff dashboards. You will own every piece of it. Your own code, your own database, and your own live website.

A "three-tier website" just means three layers working together:

1. The pages people see in their browser (the front end).
2. The behind-the-scenes code that handles requests (the server).
3. The database that remembers everything (users, listings, receipts, donations).

You do not build any of this from scratch. The project is already written for you. Your job is to get your own copy running, put it online, change a few files to make it yours, connect your own database, and then operate the finished dashboards.

## Read these in order

1. **[Prerequisites](./Prerequisites.md)**: do this at home before the first session. Create your accounts and install a few tools.
2. **[Part 1: Introduction and Architecture](./Part-1-Introduction-and-Architecture.md)**: what you are building and how the pieces fit together.
3. **[Part 2: Build the Storefront](./Part-2-Build-the-Storefront.md)** (Session 1): fork, run, deploy, and customize your catalog.
4. **[Part 3: Provision Your Backend](./Part-3-Provision-Your-Backend.md)** (Session 2): create your database and connect it so forms save real data.
5. **[Part 4: Operate the Business](./Part-4-Operate-the-Business.md)** (Session 3): set up your staff accounts, run the dashboards, and ship your final site.
6. **[Troubleshooting](./Troubleshooting.md)**: quick fixes for the snags people hit most.

## What you end each day with

| Day | You will do | You will end with |
|-----|-------------|-------------------|
| Prereqs | Create accounts, install tools, prep a GCash QR | A laptop that is ready to build |
| 1 | Fork, run, deploy, and customize your catalog | A live, personalized website at your own URL |
| 2 | Create your database, connect your keys, add your GCash QR | Sign-in works, and forms save real inquiries, receipts, and donations |
| 3 | Seed your roles, operate the dashboards, final deploy | A working site you fully own and can run |
